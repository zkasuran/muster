# Decision: no document states, implies or designs against a weighting of the three main criteria

Recorded by `00-PROGRAM.md`, from the live pages on 2026-09-05.

## The decision

The main rubric's Weight column is published empty, so nothing in this set is designed against a
weighting. No surface is traded off against another to favour Functionality, Data Quality or Agent
Diversity. TermiX's rubric is published at 30/30/20/20, so that one is designed against directly.

## The alternative rejected

Pick a reading and optimise for it. A third each or Functionality first because it is listed first
or Data Quality first because it is the hardest to fake.

## Why

The rubric table on the live page carries a Weight column with no number in it for any criterion,
checked live on 2026-09-05 against the 2026-08-27 capture and unchanged. A design that only holds
under one guess about the weights loses under the other two and three judges score independently
before comparing, so a bet on one criterion is a bet three times over.

The page also states that more criteria arrive in a second phase and the timeline entry for that
phase prints `[REDACTED]`. Nothing can be tuned for a criterion nobody has published, so the only
defence is a submission that is complete rather than tuned.

The consequence is visible in the shape of the product. The four shelves, the conformance table, the
freshness strip and the receipt all get first-class surfaces. None of them is demoted to make room for
a hero surface aimed at one criterion.

## What it binds

Every document. `02-THESIS.md` section 7 answers the three criteria without ranking them.
`03-TAXONOMY.md` gives four shelves and the conformance table equal weight in the information
architecture. `13-PARTNERS.md` is the one document that maps a published weighting, because TermiX
published one.

## What would make us revisit it

BNB Chain publishing weights or publishing the phase 2 criteria during the judging window. Until
then the empty column is a fact about the programme and not an invitation to infer.
