# Decision: the cut list is fixed now and it is cut from the bottom up

Recorded by `15-SYSTEM.md` section 9, the cut line plus the if-a-day-is-lost table.

## The decision

What ships is above a written line and everything below it is cut in a published order, from the bottom.
Losing a day cuts a **block** of that list in one decision rather than one item at a time. The demo path is
rehearsed plus protected: any block that would leave it half-built is deferred instead. Two things a lost day
may never touch, a real settled payment on mainnet plus the honesty of the ship line.

Above the line, never cut: the index with its freshness on screen, the four shelves from one template, at
least one hireable row per shelf, the hire path with a real settled mainnet payment plus a recomputable
receipt, the four Altana sessions readable and revocable, the Agent Advantage Report, the status page, the
static fallback, the licence gates, the anonymous URL check plus the disclosure.

## The alternative rejected

Decide what to drop when the time runs out.

## Why

A list written under pressure protects whatever was most recently touched, which is the opposite of what a
rubric rewards. Fixing it in advance lets the order be argued when nobody is tired: the eight cut items run
from a time series nobody will miss up to a mainnet escrow job that has a labelled testnet substitute.

Cutting a block rather than an item stops the slow bleed where three half-finished features cost more than
one abandoned one.

The money branch is written for the same reason. If the float has not arrived by 03:00 on 2026-09-07 the
brokered settle runs on testnet, the escrow leg becomes the testnet loop plus the paid probes fall back to
the free assertions, with the page saying testnet where it is testnet rather than leaving a claim standing
unqualified.

## What it binds

`15-SYSTEM.md`'s build order plus its submit checklist, `13-PARTNERS.md`'s report cut order,
`03-TAXONOMY.md` plus `07-MATCHING.md` whose thinner surfaces are named cut items,
`10-DOCS-AND-POLICY.md`'s real-against-staged table which is where a cut shows up.

## What would make us revisit it

A day arriving back rather than being lost, which releases items in the same order. The two untouchables do
not move.
