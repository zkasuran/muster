# Decision: a conforming E0 row appears on the shelf. The spine's Unlocks column still stands

Recorded 2026-09-05 by `03-TAXONOMY.md` section 6.3. This is where the third pass reads the evidence
ladder differently from the spine's own table.

## The conflict

`research/SPINE.md` lines 92 to 98 give the E0 to E4 ladder an Unlocks column. Two rows in it read on
placement:

* **E2 Proven**, earned by settled jobs through Muster in that category, unlocks "shelf placement,
  full-weight feedback, category badge".
* **E4 Accountable**, E3 plus a live Altana session, unlocks "hireable over ERC-8183, top of the default
  sort".

Read literally, E2 is the gate on shelf placement. `03-TAXONOMY.md` section 6.3 puts a third-party row at
**E0 Listed** on the shelf, in the Listed band, with its hire button disabled.

The spine beats every research file on names and constants (its rule 2). It also beats
`../ARCHITECTURE.md` and the earlier passes. It does not settle a design question a third-pass document
owns. 03 owns "visibility rules including what is listed against indexed-but-hidden, what each
evidence tier unlocks on screen" by the spine's own scope line for it. So this is the deviation to
record rather than a conflict to re-litigate.

## What this pass decides

**Two different questions, two different answers, both published.**

1. **The Unlocks column governs what a tier earns.** Nothing changes there. E2 still earns the category
   badge, full-weight feedback and full standing in the ranked bands. E4 still earns the ERC-8183 escrow
   path and the top of the default sort. `05-ONBOARDING.md` and `06-QUALITY.md` cite that column for the
   ladder and stay correct.
2. **The shelf's render rule governs what a page shows.** A third-party row that passes its category
   contract on the current `contractVersion` and reached `reachabilityTier` T2 inside the freshness window
   appears in the Listed band with:
   * no category badge,
   * the score reading `provisional (n=0)`,
   * the hire button disabled, reading "not hireable through Muster yet" with the missing requirement
     named,
   * last position inside the last band.

Nothing is promoted. Every unlock in the spine's column is still withheld. What the row gets is
visibility, which the column does not speak to.

## Why

**0 of 600 uniformly sampled agents are payable by a stranger and 12 of 600 reach T2**, all twelve one
product on one host under twelve owners (`research/MEASUREMENT.md`, seed `20260905`, block 120,027,164).
Settled jobs through Muster are the only route to E2. At build close the count is whatever we have
settled ourselves.

So an E2-only shelf on 2026-09-09 is one of two things. Both lose:

* **Four empty shelves.** Functionality is scored on "land, find an agent by category, understand what it
  does, activate it, with minimal friction" and Agent Diversity on all four surfaced at equal depth. Four
  empty shelves fail both.
* **Four first-party shelves.** The main track asks for the marketplace and not a portfolio of agents.
  `research/R14-rivals.md` records judges discounting exactly that shape.

Showing a conforming third-party row with its hire button off is the third option. It is honest, because
the card names what is missing. It is also the only one of the three that puts a stranger's agent in front
of a buyer at all.

## The rejected alternative

Restrict the shelf to E2 and above, as the Unlocks column reads literally, then put every E0 and E1 row in
the off-shelf drawer.

Rejected because it converts a measured supply problem into an empty product. The drawer
already carries the rows that failed a contract. Collapsing "passed the contract, has never been hired"
into the same place as "failed DNS" throws away the distinction the conformance table exists to make.

## What the documents have to say

`03-TAXONOMY.md` section 6.3 states the split in one paragraph and points here. The decisions table in its
section 11 carries the row with this alternative named. Any document that cites the ladder for what a tier
earns is unaffected and does not need to repeat the display rule.

If the spine is edited later, the correct edit is one clause on the E0 row of its Unlocks column reading
"appears on the shelf in the Listed band, not hireable", which is the same statement in fewer words.
Until then this file is the record.
