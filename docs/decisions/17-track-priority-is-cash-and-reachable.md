# Main track first, then only the partner tracks that pay cash and are reachable

**Decision, 2026-09-06, with about two and a half days left.** The main track is the spine. The
partner effort goes to **TermiX and PancakeSwap**. Altana is dropped. AltLayer is filed but not built for.

**Why those two.** Both pay cash and both are selectable on the registration form, whose picker offers
PancakeSwap, AltLayer, TermiX or Not sure.

* **TermiX**, $6,000/$3,000/$1,000. The only fully weighted rubric published anywhere in the programme,
  so the points are predictable rather than guessed. Its Agent Advantage Report is an eligibility gate,
  not a bonus, so it is a build deliverable.
* **PancakeSwap**, 1,000 CAKE. Its ask lands inside two shelves we already have to build well, since
  rebalancing and yield both read PancakeSwap pools.

**Why Altana is dropped despite 50,000 XP.** Three reasons compounding, none of them enough alone. It pays XP rather than cash, which is the operator's stated priority. **The registration form has no
Altana option**, so there is no published route to claim it from this entry. And it is the most expensive
track left: self-custodial agent wallets, sessions carrying a call allowlist plus a spend cap plus an
expiry, registered in Keystore so the grant is read on chain, live transactions in their explorer, plus
revocation visible in the product. Binance's own Agentic Wallet cannot stand in for it, because that is
MPC custodial with server-side limits, no expiry and no on-chain permission record. Building it properly
costs a day we do not have, while building it badly scores nothing on a track we cannot even tick.

**Why AltLayer is filed anyway.** Their reward is 8004scan Pro plus AltLLM credits rather than cash, yet
we read 8004scan as our cross-check index regardless. The Pro-tier form is minutes and the key raises our
own rate limits, so it pays for itself whether or not it scores.

**What this costs if it is wrong.** 50,000 Altana XP, plus the on-chain session work that would have been
the strongest verifiable evidence in the submission. The trade is accepted because the main track is
$30,000 plus adoption and is scored on three criteria that all need depth we would have taken from.

**Revisit if:** the organisers confirm a project-submission channel that does accept an Altana wallet
address, failing that the main track hits its gates early enough on day three that sessions become additive rather
than subtractive.
