# Decision: the product is called Muster

Recorded by `02-THESIS.md` section 2 plus its decisions table.

## The decision

The product ships as **Muster**. A muster is the assembly where everyone present is counted and
checked, which is what the product does to the registry.

## The alternative rejected

A descriptive name in the `bnb-agent-marketplace` shape, which is what a judge scanning a submission
list expects to see.

## Why

Fourteen rival repositories hold that exact descriptive name, so a judge scanning titles merges them
into one impression. Of the distinctive names in the same census, four are a form of `mandate` and
three of `assay`, so those are taken as well. A name that collides is a name that costs recall in the
one place recall is free.

The name also has to survive being said aloud in a live conversation, spelled once, then typed into a
URL bar without a hyphen. It carries no claim we would have to defend.

## What it binds

Every outward surface: the repo, the domain, the docs set, the policy documents that name the venue as
a party, the coverage plus report pages, the submission.

## What would make us revisit it

A trademark conflict or an adoption conversation where BNB Chain wants the venue under its own brand.
The adoption note is a separate instrument and the licence posture already anticipates it
(`10-docs-the-licence-split.md`), so a rename would be a term of that agreement rather than a design
change.
