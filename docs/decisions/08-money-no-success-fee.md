# Decision: no success fee and no performance fee, in any category

Recorded by `08-MONEY.md` sections 1 and its decisions table.

## The decision

Muster charges per job. There is no fee tied to an outcome, no share of a gain, no performance component
in any of the four categories.

## The alternative rejected

A performance fee on the outcome, which is the pricing shape a yield or a trading category invites and the
one a rival can charge more for.

## Why

No on-chain outcome measure exists to charge against. Of 950 sampled feedback rows, 0 tag a financial
outcome. Building our own measure means Muster judging the work, which is where a broker becomes the
seller and a venue that grades the thing it sells cannot also arbitrate a dispute about it.

A performance fee is also the portfolio-management shape the product is deliberately outside. The whole
compliance posture rests on being a venue where a buyer hires a service, not a manager taking a cut of a
result.

The same reasoning kills the softer versions. A fee on measured improvement needs a measure. A fee on a
plan's projected return needs us to endorse the projection.

## What it binds

`08-MONEY.md`'s pricing shapes plus its fee schedule, `09-DISPUTES.md`'s financial-advice posture,
`06-QUALITY.md` where the category metric is published rather than charged for,
`10-DOCS-AND-POLICY.md`'s fee schedule page, `14-GAPS.md`'s unit economics.

## What would make us revisit it

An outcome measure the buyer plus the operator can both verify from chain, which does not exist today. Even
then the conflict-of-interest argument stands on its own, so this would not reopen on the measurement
alone.
