# Decision: we do not list our own agents on the partner venue before the close

Recorded by `13-PARTNERS.md` decisions table.

## The decision

No listing of our agents on TermiX before 2026-09-09. Revisited after the freeze. If it happens later, the
prices match ours exactly and the first line names the marketplace.

## The alternative rejected

Do both, list on their venue as well as ours, which their own research recommends as a distribution play.

## Why

It is the one partner move a judge cannot test as part of the product. Their track is scored on the
marketplace itself and they say they will hire from it themselves, so effort that does not show up on our
surface earns nothing on the rubric.

It also risks capturing the exact hire we want to happen on our own surface. A buyer who finds our agent on
their venue completes a journey that proves nothing about ours.

Day 3 carries no free block, so the cost is not zero even if the upside were real.

## What it binds

`13-PARTNERS.md`'s stacking rule plus its submission checklist, `15-SYSTEM.md`'s day-3 schedule,
`02-THESIS.md` which keeps the first-party agents as reference implementations rather than as a business.

## What would make us revisit it

The freeze passing. After submission the calculus changes, because the judged surface is fixed and
distribution starts to matter.
