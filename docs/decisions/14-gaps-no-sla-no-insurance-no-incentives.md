# Decision: no SLA, no insurance, no referral reward, no points

Recorded by `14-GAPS.md` sections 7.1, 7.2 and 7.4. Three Refused verdicts with one shape.

## The decision

No availability target in the terms plus no uptime credits, replaced by a published freshness contract and
a public canary. No insurance plus no venue-funded coverage pool, while the operator bond stays a
specification with no deployment (`05-onboarding-operatorbond-not-deployed.md`). No referral reward, no
points, no token plus no paid placement.

## The alternative rejected

A 99.x percent availability target in the terms. A pooled fund the venue tops up so a buyer is covered when
an agent fails. Paying whoever routed a hire, which comparable designs elsewhere do.

## Why

There is no billing relationship to credit against, because the fee is per job at settlement. An
availability promise with no remedy is a misrepresentation, whereas a `live` row going `stale` after 15
minutes is a behaviour a reader can verify.

There is no underwriter for an autonomous agent's output. A venue-funded pool is an unlicensed insurance
product plus a custody surface, which bolts two regulated activities onto a product that currently has
none. A bond is the seller's own money at risk. A pool is ours.

On incentives the regulator's own handbook bars offering a retail client any monetary or non-monetary
incentive, with referral bonuses named in the guidance and undisclosed paid ranking is unfair in all
circumstances under the consumer rules. A disclosed paid slot still poisons the one signal this product
has, which is a job somebody paid for.

## What it binds

`14-GAPS.md`'s register, `09-DISPUTES.md`'s remedy funders, `05-ONBOARDING.md`'s bond,
`07-matching-ties-break-against-our-own-agents.md` for the placement half,
`10-DOCS-AND-POLICY.md` which publishes each refusal as a term rather than as silence.

## What would make us revisit it

A licensed entity plus a billing relationship, which is what the entry-to-an-operated-product section
treats as a later phase. A deployed bond would change the remedy funding without changing any of the
three refusals.
