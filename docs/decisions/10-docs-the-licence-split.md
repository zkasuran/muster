# Decision: the entry ships source-available with no derivatives. The kit ships Apache-2.0

Recorded by `10-DOCS-AND-POLICY.md` section 6.

## The decision

Split by whether an artifact is an entry or a contribution.

* **Entry parts under SAND-1.0**, source-available with no derivatives: the marketplace, the four
  category surfaces, the policy prose plus the docs. It grants running for any purpose, reading,
  decompiling, benchmarking then publishing findings. It withholds redistribution plus derivative works.
* **The compliance layer plus the conformance kit under Apache-2.0 immediately.**
* **The four reference agents under SAND-1.0 with a published change date of 2026-09-24 to Apache-2.0.**
* An `ADOPTION.md` beside the SAND `LICENSE`, naming what an adoption grant would have to add.

## The alternative rejected

MIT or Apache-2.0 across the whole repository to look cooperative. Also rejected: SAND on everything,
including the kit. Also rejected: Apache-2.0 on the reference agents immediately. Also rejected:
pre-licensing the entry permissively in case adoption needs it.

## Why

A rival with empty shelves can fork four category surfaces plus paste our policy set and a field that
publishes no terms at all can clear a marketplace-quality bar with ours. Permissive terms on an entry are
the permission to re-register our work as a competing entry. SAND still grants every verification a judge
wants, so nothing about scrutiny is withheld.

The kit is the opposite case. Neither the compliance layer nor the conformance suite is a shelf and both
gain value from adoption: if rivals run our assertions our definitions become the ecosystem's, then a
safer venue is worth more to us than exclusivity over a probe list. Apache rather than MIT for the patent
grant plus the `NOTICE` requirement.

The reference agents are the timing case. Four working agents in the four mandated categories, published
permissively four days before the close, is a shelf-filling kit for a rival whose own README concedes
empty coverage. The dated change hands the ecosystem the same benefit the moment the taking risk ends,
published up front so it is a term rather than a favour.

Pre-licensing for adoption hands the same rights to every rival at the same moment, for a prize nobody has
awarded. Adoption needs run, host plus modify rights, which is a separate instrument.

## What it binds

`10-DOCS-AND-POLICY.md`'s licence table plus its publish gate, `13-PARTNERS.md`'s upstream skill
publication, `15-SYSTEM.md`'s pre-submit gates, every repo that flips public at submission.

## What would make us revisit it

The change date arriving for the reference agents, which is already published. An adoption agreement,
which would be its own grant with its own terms rather than a change to this one.
