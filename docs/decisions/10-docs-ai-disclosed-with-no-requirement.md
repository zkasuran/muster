# Decision: AI assistance is disclosed even though no rule requires it

Recorded by `10-DOCS-AND-POLICY.md` section 5.1, with `00-PROGRAM.md` recording the absence of a rule.

## The decision

The submission plus the public repository README state that AI assistance was used, what it did and what
was verified. The disclosure ships whether or not the programme asks for one.

## The alternative rejected

Wait to read the programme's own terms before deciding, since neither the hackathon page nor the launch
blog states a disclosure rule and the full terms sit behind a WAF that refuses an automated read.

## Why

The asymmetry decides it. Disclosing where it was not required costs nothing. Not disclosing where it
turns out to be required is disqualification, on a $30,000 track plus an adoption decision.

The wording carries the same honesty floor as the rest of the entry. It says what was verified rather than
implying a review that did not happen and it does not claim a human read code no human read.

The absence of a rule is itself recorded as **unverified** rather than as permission, because the terms
could not be read.

## What it binds

`10-DOCS-AND-POLICY.md`'s disclosure document, the submission itself, the public README,
`15-SYSTEM.md`'s pre-submit checklist.

## What would make us revisit it

Reading the actual terms, which could only make the disclosure required rather than forbidden. Nothing
would make it come out.
