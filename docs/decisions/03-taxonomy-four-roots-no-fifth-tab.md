# Decision: exactly four roots, twelve sub-capabilities, no fifth tab

Recorded by `03-TAXONOMY.md` sections 3 and 3.1.

## The decision

Four category roots and nothing else in the top-level navigation: rebalancing, grid, yield, health
factor. Three sub-capabilities under each root, twelve shipped, two more documented as next. The rest
of the registry, which is 99.8 percent of it, is reachable by search plus a supporting-reads facet
rather than by a shelf of its own.

## The alternative rejected

A fifth shelf for the long tail or a general shelf, which is where every agent that does not fit
would otherwise go. Also rejected: shipping all fourteen sub-capabilities or leaving the tree
unbalanced at two, two, four, four.

## Why

A fifth tab competes for the attention the rubric points at the four and the rubric is explicit that
single-category submissions score poorly with all four at equal depth as the bar. The tail is reachable
without diluting a shelf.

Sub-capability counts are the same argument one level down. Agent Diversity scores equal depth, so an
unequal count is asymmetry in exactly the scored dimension. Twelve symmetric is more depth than eight
symmetric. The two left out each reach outside their own contract, so including them would break the
one-contract-one-listing rule.

The programme's own materials moved between the launch blog's four (monitoring, grid, health factor,
yield) and the live rubric's four (rebalancing, grid, yield, health factor). We build to the rubric
page. Monitoring is not one of the four.

## What it binds

`03-TAXONOMY.md`'s navigation, `02-THESIS.md` section 8 on equal depth as a design constraint,
`06-QUALITY.md`'s per-category metric, `07-MATCHING.md`'s per-category candidate sets,
`13-PARTNERS.md`'s three report tasks drawn from three of the four contracts, `15-SYSTEM.md`'s build
order which builds the four shelves from one template.

## What would make us revisit it

The programme mandating a fifth category or the tail proving to hold a cluster deep enough to earn its
own contract after judging. The supporting-reads facet is a holding pattern rather than an answer, so
that question stays open on purpose.
