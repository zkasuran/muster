# Decision: other teams' agents are listed and hired at their published price

Recorded by `02-THESIS.md` sections 9 and its decisions table.

## The decision

Agents built by other teams in this same programme are listed on our shelves, ranked by the same
published formula as everything else, hired at the price they published, on mainnet. What comes back is
published, including a refusal or silence. We say nothing about them we did not read from chain or from
their own endpoint.

## The alternative rejected

Exclude programme rivals from the shelves, on the reasoning that hiring a competitor's agent
strengthens their entry.

## Why

The registry is public and `createJob` names any provider address, so their agents are listable
whether or not they cooperate. The practice is already happening in the field: one marketplace funded a
BSC testnet job against an agent operated by another marketplace. Excluding rivals would shrink real
supply to protect a ranking we claim is neutral, which is the opposite of the claim.

The guardrail is the third-party label plus the honesty rule. Two of these agents publish live prices
of 0.10 `$U` and 0.25 `$U`, so the hire is affordable and the result is evidence either way. One
caveat travels with one of them: its provider address equals its client address on every job sampled,
which is a self-hire rather than a market, so its record is shown that way and weighted that way.

Nothing in the rules we read addresses whether hiring a rival's agent during the programme draws an
objection, so that is recorded as **unverified** rather than assumed either way.

## What it binds

`03-TAXONOMY.md`'s third-party label, `06-QUALITY.md`'s treatment of a single-client record,
`07-MATCHING.md`'s neutrality rules, `02-thesis-brainonbnb-is-third-party.md` for the ownership test
that keeps a rival cluster out of our own counts.

## What would make us revisit it

The programme publishing a rule against it. A rival agent that takes payment then delivers nothing
does not change the decision: that outcome is published like any other, which is what the record is
for.
